## VERDICT (Fable, 2026-06-10)

**Question asked:** "Does COT actually help my EAs, and can we finally measure it
outside the Strategy Tester's WebRequest ban?"

**Answers, with the receipts above:**

1. **Your API's sign rule WORKS as a filter.** Across 9 pairs on the identical
   trend strategy it lifts mean PF from 0.98 → 1.28 and cuts average drawdown
   roughly in half. It earns its place as an *optional, default-ON* filter for
   trend EAs on metals + USD majors.
2. **The COT-index (3y percentile of spec net) is the best variant on metals**
   (XAUUSD PF 1.41→1.53 with similar return, EURUSD 0.94→1.70, EURJPY 1.05→2.05)
   and is the mode shipped as default in AdaptiveSwingTrader_v2.
3. **The commercials-net rule is HARMFUL** (mean PF 0.84 < no-filter 0.98) —
   exactly as the prior session found for metals. It is not offered in any v2/v3 EA.
4. **Positioning extremes are momentum-CONFIRMING, not contrarian, on gold**
   (80-100 bucket: +1.61%/4w, t=11.0) **and USDJPY** (+1.13%/4w, t=6.9).
   **GBP inverts** (extremes fade, t≈−2.6) — so GBPUSD is excluded from the
   default COT-gated basket.
5. **The tester problem is solved structurally**: `MQL5-ENHANCED/FableCOT.mqh`
   embeds the real publish-lagged weekly history (2005→2026, 9 assets,
   1118 weeks each, +3-day publish lag) so every COT mode above can be
   backtested and OPTIMIZED in the Strategy Tester with zero WebRequest.

**Caveats (so nobody over-believes):** FX price data ends 2020 (gold runs to
2025); the trend strategy itself was chosen before this A/B but shares the
family of the prior session's strategy; the filter *delta* (gate vs no gate on
identical trades) is the trustworthy number here, not the absolute returns.
Forward-validate on demo like everything else.
