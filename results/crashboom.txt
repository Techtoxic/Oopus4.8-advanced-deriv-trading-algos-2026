INTERPRETATION
  * Direction is lopsided but DRIFT ≈ 0: the rare spike pays back every small tick.
  * Spike arrival is memoryless => you cannot time it; 'overdue' is the gambler's fallacy.
  * Deriv lists ONLY Accumulator + Multiplier on Crash/Boom (no binary Rise/Fall).
    - Multiplier: zero-drift underlying => pre-cost EV 0; commission/spread => EV<0.
    - Accumulator: the spike is exactly the barrier breach that zeroes your growth.
