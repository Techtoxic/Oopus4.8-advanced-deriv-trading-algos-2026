RISK OF RUIN — the 'guaranteed $1/day' martingale, simulated honestly
==========================================================================================

### Even/Odd martingale, $1/day, $10 start (1 week)
    contract win prob 0.500, payout 1.950, start $10.00, target $1.00/day, horizon 7 days
    P(win any single day)         ~ 0.8750
    P(RUIN within 7 days)         =  62.88%
    P(RUIN within  7 days)        =  62.88%
    median final bankroll         = $   0.00   (the 'it works!' illusion)
    MEAN  final bankroll          = $   4.47   (the truth: EV)
    expected P/L over  7 days     = $  -5.53
