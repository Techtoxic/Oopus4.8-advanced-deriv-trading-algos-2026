## 1. THE ONE EDGE THAT EVER EXISTED, AND HOW IT DIED

**Mechanism.** Digit concentration is driven by sigma_pips = the per-tick standard deviation
measured in units of the last decimal place. When sigma_pips is large the last digit is
uniform. When it falls below ~4.5 the digit distribution becomes non-uniform and the fixed
payout grid no longer matches it.

JD100's spot decays (volatility drag), and its pip_size is frozen at 0.01. So sigma_pips
fell with spot, and the instrument drifted into a regime where the grid was stale. That is
the entire edge: **a parameter that drifted out of calibration, not a pattern in the data.**

**Validated.** sigma = 0.03982 + 1.746764e-04 * spot, R^2 = 99.8% of the ceiling set by the
estimator's own noise (residual/noise = 1.01). Refit on 46 days: 0.06887 + 1.735087e-04 *
spot, R^2 0.9534 over spot 186-272. The gate spot is stable to 0.2% between fits.

The wrapped-normal model predicts the digit pmf from sigma alone, parameter-free, and was
confirmed across FOUR symbols spanning a 44x sigma range (3.7 to 161) with per-symbol
residuals under 0.002.

**How it died.** Deriv cut the JD100 payout four times in eight days:

    2026-08-04  1.953 -> 1.818   breakeven 51.20% -> 55.01%
    2026-08-12  1.818 -> 1.810
    2026-08-12  1.810 -> 1.794
    2026-08-13  1.794 -> 1.800

The information available at JD100's best sigma band supports 55.13%. The payout that would
make that band breakeven is 1.8139. **They pay 1.8000 — 0.8% inside the fair value of the
most informative regime in a 46-day sample.** No other symbol was cut; JD50 was audited at
$10 stake and all 22 contracts are unchanged.

    reservoir computing, and recovering the generator statistically.

If your proposal reads price history and outputs a prediction, it is already bounded.

**The one caveat, which a reviewer correctly raised**: MI is statistical, algebraic state
recovery is exact. A deterministic PRNG shows ~zero MI and is perfectly predictable. That is
addressed in section 5.

**The intermittency question, also correctly raised**: 0.0023 bits is a sample average.
Tested — windowed MI is 5-173x wider than shuffled with persistence up to 0.88. But
corr(sigma, windowed MI) = -0.87 and corr(wrapped-normal prediction, MI) = +0.974. The
intermittency IS sigma. After regressing on the correct (convex) model rather than a linear
one, persistence collapsed from +0.4176 to +0.0579.

## 3. STRUCTURAL ARGUMENTS THAT CLOSE WHOLE FAMILIES

**Linearity.** E[X+Y] = E[X] + E[Y] under ANY dependence. Every hold-to-expiry combination
therefore has EV equal to the sum of its parts, and every part carries a house margin.
Verified directly on Step Index: mixing durations gives exactly (EV_a + EV_b)/2 with no
interaction term. **This closes every hedge, straddle, multi-leg and cross-duration
structure at once.**

**Convexity (no Dutch book).** For long-only weights, E[portfolio] = sum w_i E[payoff_i].
Every contract measures a 5-11% house margin so every E[payoff_i] < 1. An arbitrage needs
payoff >= 1+eps in every state, forcing E >= 1+eps > 1. Contradiction. Deriv does not permit
shorting digit contracts, so w_i >= 0 holds. **Distribution-free — true at any sigma, after
any repricing.** Also: same-tick digit contracts are mutually exclusive outcomes of one
draw, not correlated assets, so portfolio theory does not apply.

**Volatility drag is not tradable.** JD100's log-drift is -0.186%/day, exactly -sigma^2/2,
confirmed to the decimal over 46 days (-8.2% predicted, -8.2% actual). But that is Jensen:
200k simulated paths give mean LOG return -8.42% while mean PRICE is +0.12%. E[S_T] = S_0. A
short position pays in price, so E[P&L] = 0 before costs. The measured 46-day move of -8.15%
is 0.20 sd from zero.

**Entry boundary.** Measured directly: buy at time t -> entry is tick T+1 -> settlement is
tick T+1. Horizon is exactly ONE step. This kills all latency arbitrage: a feed lead reports
a tick that has already occurred, and to act on knowing T+1 you must buy after seeing it,
which gets you entry T+2. Information arrives on the wrong side of the boundary at any lead
size.

## 4. EXPERIMENTS RUN AND WHAT THEY RETURNED

**Contract families** (surface_scan, step_units_scan, family_matrix). Runs, Asian,
HighLowTicks, Touch/NoTouch, Reset, Accumulator, Turbos, Vanilla, Expiry Range/Miss, Range —
all correctly priced. Step Index parity priced EXACTLY: 26 tie-free durations across ticks,
seconds and minutes all quote 1.9530, and tie-bearing durations track 2/(1-P_tie) where
P_tie = C(k,k/2)/2^k. They compute the binomial coefficient.

**Chart patterns** (ta_multiscan). 4 symbols x 5 timeframes x 6 strategies = 112 cells:
sr_reversal, fvg_fill, order_block, breakout, momentum, mean_revert. Each run on real ticks
AND on the same steps shuffled. **The 112 t-statistics are a standard normal** (mean +0.027,
sd 1.201, max |t| 3.4 vs an expected 3.1). Note: on a pure Gaussian random walk with zero
memory, swing highs rejected downward 77.7% of the time over 10,574 tests — white noise
produces textbook support and resistance, which is why the shuffled control is the only
meaningful comparison.

**Process memory.** No volatility clustering (|step| autocorr max 0.0057, R^2 3e-5). No
signed autocorrelation of economic size (worst 0.0106, R^2 0.0001). Runs test excess 0.54%.
Jumps are Poisson (CV 1.167, indicator autocorr 0.005 vs 0.0046 threshold) AND irrelevant:
conditioning on "next tick is not a jump" with perfect hindsight moves the win rate 0.018pp.

**Cross-symbol** (cross_symbol). 21 pairs, lags -5..+5, step correlation, sign agreement,
magnitude coupling, digit mutual information, all against block-permutation nulls. Max |z|
2.5, zero exact coincidences, detection floor r = 0.012. Independent RNG streams.

**Early close** (sellback_vol). 567 quotes on 1HZ100V priced against fair value measured
empirically from the k-tick displacement distribution. Mean bid/fair 0.8219 — a **17.81%
spread**, and it WIDENS exactly where the option would help: 0.9009 at 240-298 ticks
remaining falling to 0.6476 at 1-63, and 0.9633 deep in-the-money falling to 0.5206 deep
out-of-the-money.

**Deal cancellation** (deal_cancel, cancel_pair_live). The single leg is priced sensibly:
the stop-out already caps loss at stake, so the option cannot exceed the stake, and Deriv
charges 7.62 against a 10.00 ceiling. The paired trade looked +18.69 EV on paper. Live on
demo: with both legs open, summed P&L pinned at **-2 x commission** poll after poll — the
mult_trunc identity holds in real time. The loser auto-cancels at stop-out with a full stake
refund, the survivor runs naked, and both can knock out on a whipsaw. 3 rounds: -8.24,
-1.08, +4.49.

**Generator** (generator_probe). Longest repeated digit run 10-11 vs a 10.6 expectation.
Lempel-Ziv parse count ratio real/uniform = **0.99975** (35,873 vs 35,882). 19,143 distinct
step values. Digit marginal chi2 4.6 on 9 df. Incompressible by a universal parser, which
catches structure at any order.

**Duration** (duration_surface). Entry-digit conditioning collapses with horizon: lift
+24.77% at N=1, +4.54% at N=2, +1.01% at N=3. Every positive candidate is N=1.

**Sigma estimation** (sigma_from_spot). Replacing the rolling W=1800 RMS with the affine law
cuts RMSE 25% against a long-window reference AND removes an 1800-tick (30 min) lag. Sigma
is not a latent state — it is spot times a constant, observed exactly. Kalman/particle
filtering has nothing to separate.

**Feed asymmetry** (feed_race). Two independent sockets to the one gateway show a 93.8 ms
lead at t = 23.6, 96.7% consistent — probably a client artifact (GIL, or Cloudflare routing
to different backends). Irrelevant regardless, by the entry-boundary argument.

## 5. THE FINDING THAT EXPLAINS EVERYTHING

`info_universe.py` measured MI on 28 symbols against each one's own wrapped-normal
prediction. `trade_matrix.py` pulled contracts_for on all 89.

    step indices    MI 0.9999 - 2.3217 bits   digits offered? NO
    BOOM/CRASH      MI 0.0066 - 0.0617 bits   digits offered? NO
    1HZ / R_ / RD   MI ~0.000000              digits offered? YES
    JD100           MI 0.002469               digits offered? YES

stpRNG measures **2.321748** against log2(10) - log2(2) = 2.321928. That is
H(next|current) = 1 bit exactly: the next digit has TWO possible values (d +/- 1), so eight
of ten are impossible on the next tick. Boom/Crash carry information because they are
engineered asymmetric, giving a skewed peaked step distribution.

**Deriv sells digit contracts precisely where the digit distribution is uniform, and
withholds them where it is not.** Boom/Crash expose only ACCU/MULTUP/MULTDOWN. Step indices
expose only CALL/CALLE/PUT/PUTE/MULTUP/MULTDOWN.

JD100 was the single exception — digits offered AND sigma low enough to matter — and it
reached that state only because drag walked it there after launch.

## 6. THE PROPOSAL ENDPOINT LIES (methodology warning)

On JD100 the `proposal` endpoint serves the PRE-CUT grid. Executed buys return 1.794-1.800
on OVER4 while proposals quote 1.953. The lie is graduated: exact on low payouts, up to 25%
on the tails (MATCH quoted 8.929, fills 6.349). Confirmed independently on CALLE/PUTE
(proposal 1.9530, fill 1.800).

**This produced FIVE false positives in this project**, the most recent one hours ago: a
scan flagged JD100 OVER5 at +4.22% EV with a positive 99% lower bound. At the executed 2.185
it is -6.17%. A phone screenshot of the buy button caught it.

Any tool that reads a JD100 payout from a proposal is wrong.

## 7. ERRORS MADE, SO YOU CAN AVOID REPEATING THEM

These cost days and every one produced a spectacular fake result first.

1. **Looping fetcher.** `history_paged` silently returned the same day ~18 times (86,401
   unique epochs out of 1.6M requested) because `ticks_history` takes a `start` parameter
   that defaults to one day ago. Chi-square scales linearly with replication: a reported
   661.5 was really 36.0. Wilson intervals were too narrow by sqrt(k). Fixed in
   `derivfetch.py`, which now raises on degenerate data.

2. **Wrong functional form read as signal, three times.** Through-origin sigma fit (made a
   real intercept look like drift); raw plug-in MI at k=4 (bias (cells-1)/(2n ln2) reads
   0.0185 vs a corrected 0.0023, and at k=6 the bias alone is 1.80 bits, 200x breakeven);
   linear-in-sigma regression of a convex MI(sigma) curve (manufactured a fake "second state
   variable" with 0.42 persistence that vanished to 0.058 under the correct model).

3. **Selection bias.** An entry-digit spread of 2.42pp at n=86k became 0.94pp at 400k and
   0.36pp at 1.2M — 1/sqrt(n) scaling, the signature of noise.

4. **Omitted cost.** A cancellable multiplier pair looked +21 EV because commission on
   notional was not subtracted, and the apparent edge grew with multiplier — the signature
   of a forgotten notional-scaled cost.

5. **Guessing API shapes instead of reading source or docs.** Invented two gateway URLs
   (both failed); added `currency` to `contracts_for` (fails validation, and the error was
   swallowed, reporting 89/89 symbols as broken).

**Every scan must carry a shuffled or permutation control. Every payout must come from an
executed buy. Every fit must be checked for functional-form misspecification.**

## 8. WHAT WOULD ACTUALLY BE NEW

Do not propose: anything that is a function of past ticks; any hedge or combination; any
Dutch book; any portfolio optimisation; any latency play; anything requiring raw generator
output (we observe rounded cumulative sums at ~4-6 bits per tick, so MT19937 inverse
tempering cannot run — it would fail whether or not the generator is MT).

Genuinely open, in descending order of plausibility:

1. **A stale parameter somewhere else.** This is the only shape of edge that has ever worked
   here. Something whose calibration assumed a value that has since drifted — a barrier
   defined in absolute rather than relative terms, a duration boundary, a rounding rule, a
   grid that has not been updated as an instrument aged. JD100 was exactly this.

2. **Mechanism/documentation mismatch.** Places where implementation differs from the spec:
   settlement rules, barrier definitions, tick counting at duration boundaries, contract
   lifecycle. The Step Index off-by-one (a "15 second" contract spans 14 tick intervals) was
   found this way and was real, though correctly priced.

3. **Newly launched symbols** before their grid is tuned, or existing symbols whose sigma
   drifts into a concentrated regime while digits remain offered.

State what result would prove you wrong before proposing a test. If your honest conclusion
is that nothing remains, say so and name the argument that closes the last case — that is a
useful answer, not a failure.
