# Session 2026-08-04 — JD100 repricing, four closed axes, one live lead

## Summary

The JD100 digit book was rebuilt across every product line. The original edge is dead at the
new grid. Four new search axes were opened and closed. One genuinely new lead was found:
the win rate on the +/-2 digit window varies systematically by ENTRY DIGIT, and the ranking
survives out of sample.

## 1. The JD100 book was rebuilt, not patched

`payout_audit.py` measured EXECUTED payouts (from the buy response) against the old grid:

|---|---:|---:|---:|

| OVER7 / UNDER2 | 4.717 | 4.000 | 15.2% |
| OVER6 / UNDER3 | 3.205 | 2.857 | 10.9% |

| OVER3 / UNDER6 | 1.634 | 1.5429 | 5.8% |
| OVER0 / UNDER9 | 1.096 | 1.057 | 3.7% |

Not a payout tweak — a graduated margin restructure. House margin went from a nearly flat
~2% to 5-33%, with the biggest haircuts on the high-payout tails. That flatness was exactly
what made a 1.5% structural edge exploitable.

Breakeven on the restricted ladder moved **51.20% -> 54.69%**, a 3.7pp jump, against a best
measured live win rate of 52.78%.

### CRITICAL: the proposal endpoint serves the PRE-CUT grid

`regime_forecast.py` reported MATCH at 8.929 from proposals while execution fills at 6.657.
The lie is graduated — it vanishes on low payouts (1.057, 1.171, 1.343 all match exactly)
and reaches 25% on the tails. Confirmed independently on CALLE/PUTE: proposal 1.9530,
executed 1.800 (`tie_probe.py`), which is also a correction to FINDINGS.md sec 3, where
1.835 was recorded.

**Any tool reading payouts from proposals is reading a grid that no longer exists.**

# CORRECTION (same day, later) — most of the above was computed on LOOPED TICKS

## The bug

`deriv_api.history_paged()` silently loops. Asked for 1.6M ticks of JD100 it returned
**86,401 unique epochs — 5.44%**, the same day repeated ~18 times (min inter-tick gap
-86401s).

Cause: `ticks_history` takes an optional `start` parameter which, for `style=ticks`,
**defaults to one day ago**. Paging with only `end` set meant that once `end` moved earlier
than that implicit default, the requested window was empty and the server returned the
latest data again. Sending an explicit `start` fixes it — verified at **1,200,000 unique
ticks over 13.89 days, 100% density**.

`derivfetch.py` is now the only correct fetcher and raises rather than returning degenerate
data.

## What it invalidated

chi-square scales linearly with replication, so:

| reported | replication | true | null mean |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| randomness_battery test C = 661.5 | 18.4x | 36.0 | 81 |
| battery_v2 = 675.6 | 6.9x | 98.0 | 81 |

On clean data the entry/offset chi2 is **67.6 on 81 df** — below the null mean, consistent
with independence. **The "z = +47 dependence" was entirely replication.**

Wilson intervals were too narrow by sqrt(k): 2.1x on 400k runs, 3.0x on 800k, 3.7x on
regime_revalidate's 1.2M. IS/OOS splits put copies of the same ticks on both sides.

Only `cross_symbol.py` (60k, under the cap) was unaffected — its independence result stands
as written.

## THE ENTRY-DIGIT RESULT IS DEAD

Section 4 above reports it as a live lead. It is not. The per-digit spread scaled as
1/sqrt(n), which is the signature of noise:

| unique ticks | SE/digit | spread | noise 95th |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 86,401 | 0.537pp | 2.42pp | 2.44pp |
| 400,000 | 0.250pp | 0.94pp | 1.14pp |
| **1,200,000** | **0.144pp** | **0.36pp** | **0.66pp** |

A real 2.42pp spread would have stayed at 2.42pp. Half-A/half-B rho on clean data is
**-0.188**. Best digit EV is -3.78%.

Also note: `clean_fetch.py` printed "SURVIVES" twice because it compared the observed spread
against the MEAN noise range, which half of pure-noise samples exceed by definition. The
correct threshold is the 95th percentile. `derivfetch.noise_range_pct()` now does this.

Duration axis re-confirmed dead on clean data: N=1 at -3.78%, N>=2 at -7% to -8%.
Vol clustering flags at r=0.00566 vs a 0.00456 threshold, but that is R^2 = 0.000032.
