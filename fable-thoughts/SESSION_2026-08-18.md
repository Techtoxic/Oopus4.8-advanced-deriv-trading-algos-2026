## Hunt 3 — Accumulators (the one real discovery of the day, in three acts)

**Act 1 — a live anomaly appeared.** With the longcode KO band (±0.00025% at g=0.03),
BOOM1000 ACCU priced at **+3.8% at N=50 / +8.7% at N=90** on 500k ticks, split-half
stable (+3.97%/+3.63%). Bid tracks stake×(1+g)^ticks at ratio 0.97–1.00 (measured live).
Looked like the first genuine mispricing in months.

**Act 2 — live verification killed it.** 9 of 9 real demo ACCU buys on BOOM1000 died at
ticks 13–54 (mean ~28) against a historical S(50)=0.24 that predicts most rounds survive
50+. No sell opportunity ever materialized.

**Act 3 — root cause.** The actual KO rule is |step|/prev_spot > **tick_size_barrier**
(the `shortcode` field, e.g. 2.4506e-6 on BOOM1000 g=0.03), NOT the rounded longcode
percentage (±0.00025% ⇒ 2.5e-6). The 2% rounding gap sits exactly in the dense shoulder
of the step distribution, so the true per-tick hazard is 0.0328, not 0.0283. Recomputed
with the exact tsb: **EV@50 = −18.1%, EV@90 = −30.4%** — and the live deaths
(mean survival ~28 ticks) match the history perfectly. Verified on a contract whose full
audit trail shows the killing step (0.056 > 0.0367 band) explicitly.

**Final ACCU table (exact tsb, 500k ticks each):** every (symbol, growth-rate) cell is
negative, −2.4% to −89%. The fairest cells are ~−2.4% (CRASH500/BOOM1000 g=0.02–0.03,
short holds). H5b is now CLOSED with direct measurement, not inference: Deriv prices the
accumulator to a −2–4% margin at short horizons, widening with hold length.
